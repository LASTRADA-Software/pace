This installs Pace: pace-kv, a fast cache daemon speaking the memcached and Redis wire protocols, together with pace-cc, its compiler launcher for shared compile caching.

Both are installed into /opt/fastcached/bin and made available on your PATH.

On the next screen you can choose whether pace-kv starts automatically:

  • Start at login — runs as you, from your next login. This is the right choice for a development machine, and it is selected by default.
  • Start at boot, system-wide — runs from boot under a dedicated _fastcached account, shared by everyone on the machine.

The two are alternatives, not additions: both would listen on the same address, so if you select the system-wide service it takes precedence and the per-user one is skipped. Selecting neither installs the tools without starting anything, which you can change later with pace-kv --install-service.
